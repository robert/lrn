**Q1** (search, para 2) The Rat and the Mole are lost, but they try hard to stay brave. How do we know?

  Clue: they go on "pretending with invincible cheerfulness that they recognized an old friend in every fresh tree". Pretending to be cheerful is how you stay brave.
- ❌ _evidence_ They sing loud marching songs so that the animals in the wood can hear them.  
  Clue check: look through the first few paragraphs. Is there any singing at all?
- ❌ _evidence_ The Rat tells the Mole he knows exactly where the path home is from here.  
  Clue check: in paragraph 1 the Rat says "I don't exactly know where we are". He is honest, not boastful.
